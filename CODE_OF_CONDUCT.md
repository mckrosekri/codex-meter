# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Codex Meter and Codex Decision to be safe, respectful, and productive for people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different experience levels, languages, identities, and viewpoints.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and help move the work forward.
- Protect private information encountered in logs, screenshots, issue reports, or test data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Pressuring someone to reveal credentials, prompts, account data, or security details publicly.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional collaborative setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, releases, and project-related public spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates it.

For a private conduct report, contact the repository owner through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/mckrosekri). If the report also involves a security or privacy risk, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, with conflicts of interest avoided.
